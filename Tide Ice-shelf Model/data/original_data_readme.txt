These are the data used in papers Murray et al (2008) and King et al (2010) on the motion of Rutford Ice Stream. 
See those papers for information on the data processing. 
A short section of the raw GPS observations, processed at higher temporal resolution, was also used in the paper AÐalgeirsd¢ttir et al (2008).
Approximate location of the site was S78.141646342 W088.91234181 (-78.141646342   276.087658190) in Dec 2004.
The data are presented in 8 columns:
Column 1: Year
Column 2: Day of year (rolls into the following years)
Column 3: East (m) (Distance East of start point in metres)
Column 4: North (m) (Distance North of start point in metres)
Column 5: Up (m) (Height above start point in metres)
Column 6: Sigma E (m) (One standard deviation of East observed position in metres)
Column 7: Sigma N (m) (One standard deviation of North observed position in metres)
Column 8: Sigma Up (m) (One standard deviation of Height observed position in metres)
